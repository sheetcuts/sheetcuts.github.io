Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2, 2026

Overview

SheetCuts ("we", "our", or "us") operates the SheetCuts Chrome extension. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

What We Collect

When you sign in to SheetCuts, we collect:

  • Your Google account email address
  • Your Google account ID

This information is collected via Google OAuth and is used solely to manage your account and subscription status.

If you purchase a paid subscription, your payment is processed by Stripe. We do not store your credit card or payment details — those are handled entirely by Stripe and subject to their privacy policy (stripe.com/privacy).

We do not collect browsing data, spreadsheet content, or any information about the documents you work on.

How We Use Your Information

We use your email and Google ID to:

  • Identify your account
  • Track your free trial period
  • Verify your subscription status
  • Send transactional emails related to your subscription (e.g. payment receipts)

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties, except as described below.

Data Storage

Your account information is stored securely in Supabase, a cloud database provider. Data is stored in the United States.

Third-Party Services

SheetCuts uses the following third-party services:

  • Google OAuth — for sign-in (policies.google.com/privacy)
  • Supabase — for account and subscription storage (supabase.com/privacy)
  • Stripe — for payment processing (stripe.com/privacy)

Your Rights

You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by emailing us at sheetcuts@gmail.com. Upon request, we will delete your record from our database and cancel any active subscription.

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data under GDPR.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of the extension after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at: sheetcuts@gmail.com
