A Macabacus & think-cell alternative for Google Workspace

SheetCuts brings the Excel and PowerPoint Alt-key keyboard shortcuts you know from Office into Google Sheets and Google Slides — so you keep your muscle memory after switching to Google Workspace.

Tools like Macabacus and think-cell are powerful, but they are Microsoft Office desktop add-ins — they don't run in Google Sheets or Google Slides. If your team has moved to Google Workspace, the thing you miss most day to day is usually the keyboard speed: the Alt-key ribbon shortcuts that let you format and navigate without ever touching the mouse. That's exactly what SheetCuts restores.

Where SheetCuts wins

  • It's built for Google Workspace. Macabacus and think-cell simply don't run in Google Sheets or Slides. SheetCuts is native to the apps you actually use now.
  • It keeps your Office muscle memory. The same Alt-key sequences you used in Excel and PowerPoint work here — press Alt and an on-screen overlay shows the keys.
  • It covers Slides, not just spreadsheets. PowerPoint-style shortcuts, shape fill, and keyboard color pickers with custom hex entry.
  • It's affordable. $6/month or $49/year, versus the premium pricing of desktop Office add-ins.

Where it doesn't (yet)

Being honest: if your day depends on formula auditing (trace precedents/dependents), Goal Seek, what-if/sensitivity tables, or automated charting, the heavier finance tools do more there. SheetCuts is deliberately focused on doing one thing extremely well — the fast, keyboard-driven ribbon workflow — across both Google Sheets and Slides.
