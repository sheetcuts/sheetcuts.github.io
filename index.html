How it works

Three keystrokes, any action.

The same muscle memory you built in Excel works here. No relearning, no configuration.

1

Press Alt

On any Google Sheet or Slide, press Alt. A key-tip overlay appears showing every available shortcut at the current level.

2

Press a ribbon key

Press H for Home, N for Insert, A for Data, and so on — just like in Excel. The overlay drills into that tab's shortcuts.

3

Complete the action

Press the final key and the action fires instantly. Press Escape at any point to cancel. That's the whole flow.
